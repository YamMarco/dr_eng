| TEACHER OVERVIEW — L7

LESSON 7  —  Complete the Sentence / Complete the Answer
P10: Find the sentence → Fit the gap → Grammar check

Objective
By the end of this lesson the student completes sentence and answer formats using exact text language and correct grammar.
Key insight
""Complete the sentence" looks easy — but most students make two mistakes: they answer from memory instead of finding the exact text sentence, and they break the grammar of the starter. The answer is always in the text, and it must sound like natural English when you read the whole sentence."
Timing plan

Common mistakes to watch for
· Student writes their own words instead of the text sentence
· Student ignores the grammar of the starter — answer does not fit
· Student writes too much — the answer goes beyond what one sentence needs
· Student answers a "complete the sentence" with a full new sentence instead of completing the given one |

| THE JOB REVOLUTION
I     The world of work is changing faster than ever before. According to a recent report, over 85 million jobs will disappear in the next ten years because of automation and artificial intelligence. However, the same report predicts that 97 million new jobs will also appear. The problem is that many workers are not prepared for these new kinds of jobs.
II     Emma Clarke, a researcher at the Digital Skills Institute in London, believes that the solution lies in education. "Most schools still teach students skills for the jobs of yesterday, not the jobs of tomorrow," she says. According to Clarke, the three most important skills for the future are critical thinking, digital literacy, and communication.
III     Some governments have already begun to act. In 2023, the government of Singapore introduced a new programme that gives every adult worker 500 dollars a year to spend on training courses. More than 300,000 workers have already taken advantage of this programme. Professor Lee Min, an economist at National University of Singapore, says the results are encouraging: workers who complete at least one course a year are 35% less likely to lose their jobs. |

| THE WATER CRISIS
Access to clean water is one of the most serious problems facing the world today. According to Dr. Layla Ahmed of the Global Water Institute, over 2 billion people currently have no access to safe drinking water. "This is not only a health crisis — it is also an economic one," she explains. "Countries that cannot provide clean water lose an estimated 260 billion dollars every year in lost productivity." Dr. Ahmed argues that the most effective solution is to invest in local water treatment systems rather than relying on international aid. |
| --- |
| T1.  Complete the sentence — How many people currently have no access to safe drinking water?
Complete. |
| --- |
| T2.  According to Dr. Layla Ahmed, what does she argue is the most effective solution to the water crisis?
Complete the answer. |
| --- |
| ANSWER KEY
T1: 2 billion people.
P2: number 2 billion → answer.
T2: ...to invest in local water treatment systems rather than relying on international aid.
P3: Dr. Ahmed → her argument. P5: most effective → one solution. |
