| מסך 2 | THE TOOL — 4 צעדים + 2 כללי זהב |
| --- | --- |
| צעד 1 | 📖 קרא את כל 4 האפשרויות לפני שחוזרים לטקסט. |
| --- | --- |
| צעד 2 | ✂️  חצה כל אפשרות שסותרת את הטקסט — או שנכונה אבל לא קשורה לשאלה. |
| צעד 3 | 🔍 עבור לטקסט. מצא הוכחה בפסקה הנכונה. |
| צעד 4 | ✅ ענה — הקף את המספר. רק אחד. |
| 💡 איך לחצות בצעד 2 — שני סוגי טעויות:

סוג א׳: אפשרות שסותרת את הטקסט ← חצה מיד.
   דוגמה: הטקסט "without payment" ← "Volunteers receive payment" ← חצה.

סוג ב׳: אפשרות שנכונה — אבל עונה על שאלה אחרת ← חצה!
   דוגמה: שאלה על פסקה I. האפשרות נכונה — אבל מדברת על פסקה III ← חצה.
   כלל: נכון + לא קשור לשאלה = שגוי. |
| --- |
| 🎯 כלל זהב ל-"What do we learn from paragraph X?":

לך ONLY לפסקה X. אל תקרא פסקאות אחרות.

השאלה מגבילה אותך לפסקה אחת בלבד. כל מידע מפסקה אחרת = שגוי, גם אם נכון בטקסט. |

| מסך 3 | WATCH IT — MC בפעולה |
| --- | --- |
| 📖 על מה הטקסט:
GIVING TIME, GAINING HAPPINESS
מתנדבים. מחקרים: מתנדבים מאושרים. ד"ר אוקאפור — בריאות נפשית. בתי ספר — תלמידים אחראים. |
| --- |
| I Every year, millions of young people around the world choose to volunteer — to give their time to help others without payment. A study found that young people who volunteer for at least two hours a week are 60% more likely to describe themselves as happy. Researchers were surprised because they expected that money and success would be the main reason for happiness.

II The benefits of volunteering go beyond simple happiness. Dr. Sarah Okafor studied the effects of volunteering on mental health for ten years. She found that teenagers who volunteer feel less stressed and sleep better. However, not all types of volunteering produce the same results. According to Dr. Okafor, the most effective programmes bring young people face to face with the people they help.

III Results from 12 countries show that schools that introduced volunteering programmes found that students became more responsible and more focused in class. Professor David Mills argues that helping others teaches young people skills that no classroom can ever replace. |
| --- |
| ❓ השאלה:
What do we learn from paragraph I about young volunteers? |
| --- |
| i) Volunteers receive payment. | ❌ סוג א׳: סותר — "without payment" |
| --- | --- |
| ii) Young volunteers are happier. | ✅ בפסקה I + הוכחה: "60% more likely to be happy" |
| iii) Researchers expected volunteering to help. | ❌ סוג א׳: סותר — "Researchers were surprised" |
| iv) Schools that volunteer have better results. | ❌ סוג ב׳: נכון — אבל זה מפסקה III, לא פסקה I! |
| ✅  התשובה: ii — הוכחה מפסקה I בלבד. |

| I Every year, forests around the world are destroyed by fire, farming, and pollution. However, there is good news. In 2019, a group of scientists started a project to plant one billion trees in Africa by 2030. The project, called Green Africa, has already planted over 200 million trees in 15 countries.

II The project is led by Dr. Amara Diallo, a scientist from Senegal. "We do not just plant trees," says Dr. Diallo. "We teach local people how to care for them." According to Dr. Diallo, most trees die because nobody looks after them. When local people are involved, 85% of trees survive.

III The results are already visible. In Ethiopia, the number of birds increased by 60% in areas where trees were planted. In addition, in Kenya, rivers that were dry for 20 years began to flow again. Scientists say that if the project continues, it could reduce carbon in the atmosphere by 15%. |

| What do we learn from paragraph II about the project?
Circle the correct answer.

◉ i)  The project teaches people to plant and care for trees.
○ ii)  Dr. Diallo plants trees personally.
○ iii) Most trees survive without any help.
○ iv)  In Ethiopia, birds increased by 60%. ← נכון — אבל מפסקה III! |
