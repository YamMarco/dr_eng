| TEACHER OVERVIEW — L13

LESSON 13  —  Vocabulary: Social Topics
Notice → Understand → Adapt → Use
Community · Education · Health · Volunteering

Objective
By the end of this lesson the student uses 20 key words from social and community topics in context, and can identify them in reading texts.
Key insight
"The 90-word bank is built lesson by lesson. Students who learn words in context (in a sentence, not from a list) remember them twice as long. Every word today appears in real Module C questions — either in the text or in the writing prompt."
Timing plan

Common mistakes to watch for
· Student memorizes translation but cannot use the word in a sentence
· Student confuses "benefit" (noun: an advantage) with "benefit from" (verb phrase: to gain from)
· Student uses "volunteer" without knowing it can be noun or verb: "a volunteer" / "to volunteer"
· Student confuses "community" (a local group) with "country" — different scale |

| Word | Hebrew | In a question | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| community | קהילה | The local community volunteered. | People in a local area. "community centre" = מרכז קהילתי. |
| volunteer | מתנדב/ת / להתנדב | She volunteers twice a week. | Noun AND verb. "a volunteer" / "to volunteer." |
| responsible | אחראי/ת | Students became more responsible. | Taking care of what you should. |
| programme | תוכנית | The training programme lasted two years. | British spelling. US: "program." |
| opportunity | הזדמנות | Volunteering is a great opportunity. | A chance to do something positive. |
| TEACHER
After the 5 main words: run the Notice→Understand→Adapt→Use sequence for each word.
NOTICE: "Here is the word in the text: 'The local community volunteered.'" Students underline.
UNDERSTAND: "What does community mean here? Not a country. Not a class. A group of local people."
ADAPT: "How would you say this about a school topic? 'Our school community raised money for...'"
USE: "Write one sentence using 'community' about a topic from real life." Students write. |
