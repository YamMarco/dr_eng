| TEACHER OVERVIEW — L22

LESSON 22  —  The Example Bank + Exam Conditions
General examples that work for any topic
Count every time: 70–90 words exactly

Objective
By the end of this lesson the student produces a complete 70–90 word writing response using the full P14 structure under exam conditions.
Key insight
"The example bank solves the "I do not know what example to use" problem. Five general sentences can be adapted to work for volunteering, jobs, language, tests, or famous speakers. They do not have to be true. They have to be relevant and grammatically correct."
Timing plan

Common mistakes to watch for
· Student writes 55 words — not enough. Add to reason 2.
· Student writes 105 words — too many. Cut the third reason.
· Student writes "Many people" without finishing the sentence — vague
· Student uses the same example twice — use one per reason |
