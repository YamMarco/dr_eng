| TEACHER OVERVIEW — L16

LESSON 16  —  Sentences that Work — Subject + Verb, Every Time
Every sentence needs a subject and a verb
Build: word → phrase → sentence → paragraph

Objective
By the end of this lesson the student writes 3–4 correct English sentences on any exam topic without missing a verb or mixing up singular and plural.
Key insight
"The 5 most expensive grammar errors in the writing task all come from the same problem: the student did not check whether the sentence has a subject AND a verb. A sentence without a verb is not a sentence. This lesson makes that check automatic."
Timing plan

Common mistakes to watch for
· Student writes "Many students jobs after school" — no verb
· Student writes "Students is good" — plural needs "are"
· Student writes "I was think that" — continuous tense error
· Student writes "The teenagers" — in general statements, no "the"
· Student writes "Because sport" — because needs a full clause |

| Word | Hebrew | In a question | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I think / I believe | אני חושב / מאמין | I think that volunteering is important. | Start your opinion sentence here. |
| because | כי / מפני ש | ...because it teaches responsibility. | Because + FULL SENTENCE — not just a noun. |
| for example | לדוגמה | For example, many students volunteer. | Starts your example. Never "for example that". |
| in addition / also | בנוסף / גם | In addition, working improves confidence. | Adds a second reason. |
| in conclusion | לסיכום | In conclusion, I believe that... | End sentence. Optional. |
