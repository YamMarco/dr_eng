"Eye Catcher: NOT" |

| המיקרו-סקיל:
ראיתי NOT? אני מחפש מה לא נכון — לא מה נכון. |
| --- |
| מסך 1 | WOW MOMENT |
| --- | --- |
| 💀 המילה הכי מסוכנת בבחינה:


NOT


90% מהתלמידים שרואים NOT בשאלה עונים על השאלה ההפוכה.
הם מחפשים מה נכון — במקום מה לא נכון.
NOT משנה את כל השאלה. |
| --- |
| מסך 2 | THE TOOL — NOT = ציד |
| --- | --- |
| שאלה רגילה:

"What do we learn?"

← מחפש מה נכון. |  | שאלה עם NOT:

"Which is NOT true?"

← מחפש מה לא נכון. |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🎯 הכלל:
כל שאר האפשרויות ← נכונות בטקסט.
האפשרות ה"לא נכונה" = התשובה. |
| --- |
| מסך 3 | WATCH IT |
| --- | --- |
| I Learning to swim as an adult is more common than many people think. According to a recent survey, over 30% of adults in large cities cannot swim. Many of them feel embarrassed about this and never try to learn. However, experts say it is never too late.

II One study found that adults who learned to swim later in life often became stronger swimmers than those who learned as children. "Adults understand what the teacher explains and can correct their mistakes more quickly," says Dr. Anna Klein. "They are also not afraid to ask questions."

III Adult swimming classes are now available in most cities. Some programs meet only once a week, while others offer three sessions a week. According to instructors, adults who practice regularly usually make good progress in less than two months. |
| --- |
| ❓ השאלה:
Which of the following is NOT mentioned in the text? |
| --- |
| i) 30% of adults cannot swim | ✅ מוזכר — פסקה I |
| --- | --- |
| ii) Adults become stronger swimmers | ✅ מוזכר — פסקה II |
| iii) The cost of adult swimming classes | ❌ לא מוזכר! ← התשובה |
| ✅  התשובה: iii — המחיר לא מוזכר. |
| --- |
