| TEACHER OVERVIEW — L20

LESSON 20  —  YES or NO — Decide Fast
Binary choice: no maybe, no both sides
Your opinion in 30 seconds — then explain it

Objective
By the end of this lesson the student makes a clear YES or NO decision on any exam writing topic in under 30 seconds and understands why both sides is not an option.
Key insight
"Students who write "on one hand... on the other hand... so there are many opinions" lose marks. The examiner is not asking for a debate. They are asking for YOUR opinion. Take a side. Defend it. Done."
Timing plan

Common mistakes to watch for
· Student writes "both sides are important" — not acceptable in Module C writing
· Student takes too long to decide — more than 60 seconds on YES/NO means they will not finish
· Student changes their opinion halfway through the paragraph
· Student writes a reason but does not state their opinion clearly first |

| Word | Hebrew | In a question | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I think / I believe | אני חושב/ת | I think that volunteering is good. | Start every opinion with this. |
| In my opinion | לדעתי | In my opinion, tests are too frequent. | More formal version. |
| because | כי | ...because it teaches responsibility. | Your reason follows immediately. |
| important | חשוב/ה | It is important for teenagers to work. | Use it to show why you care. |
| agree / disagree | מסכים/ה / לא מסכים/ה | I agree that work teaches skills. | Use to respond to the prompt directly. |
| 15–45 min | Binary choice + First sentence |
| --- | --- |
| YES — I agree because...
Take the YES side. Give 2 reasons why YES is right. Give 1 example. | NO — I disagree because...
Take the NO side. Give 2 reasons why NO is right. Give 1 example. |
| --- | --- |
| ! Never write "both sides". Never write "it depends". Never write "on one hand... on the other hand..."
The examiner wants YOUR opinion. Take a side. Defend it.
אין "שני הצדדים". אין "זה תלוי". בחר צד. הגן עליו. |

| --- |
| ANSWER KEY
Sample YES: "I think that all students should do volunteer work because it teaches them important life skills."
Sample NO: "I do not think all students should do volunteer work because some students have very little free time."
Both are correct — what matters is: clear opinion + clear because + clear reason. |
