| TEACHER OVERVIEW — L9

LESSON 9  —  Reference Questions — What Does "it / they / this" Refer To?
P7: Pronoun in question → look back one sentence → find the noun → test it

Objective
By the end of this lesson the student identifies what a pronoun or reference word refers to by looking at the sentence immediately before it.
Key insight
""What does 'it' refer to?" — 90% of the time the answer is the closest noun in the sentence before the pronoun. Students who scan the whole paragraph lose time and find the wrong answer. P7 is a two-second strategy: look one sentence back. Find the noun. Test it by substituting."
Timing plan

Common mistakes to watch for
· Student looks at the whole paragraph instead of just the sentence before the pronoun
· Student chooses a noun that is in the same sentence as the pronoun — usually wrong
· Student does not do the substitution test — they guess the nearest-sounding word
· Student confuses "this" (usually refers to an idea) with "it" (usually refers to a thing) |

| THE EXERCISE MYTH
I     Most people believe that to get fit, they need to exercise for at least 30 minutes every day. However, new research suggests this idea may be wrong. According to a study published in the Journal of Sports Science, short bursts of intense exercise — as little as seven minutes a day — can be just as effective as longer workouts. This finding challenges everything most fitness experts have said for decades.
II     Dr. Jennifer Cho, a sports scientist at Yonsei University in South Korea, tested two groups of volunteers for 12 weeks. The first group exercised for 30 minutes every day. The second group did seven minutes of intense exercise three times a day. At the end, Dr. Cho found that both groups had similar improvements. However, the second group reported feeling more energetic and less stressed.
III     Not all scientists agree with these findings. Professor Robert Walsh of Oxford University argues that the research has serious limitations. "The study only looked at healthy young adults," he says. "We cannot apply these results to older people or those with health problems." He also points out that most people cannot maintain these routines for more than a few weeks. |

| TEACHER — I DO
I DO — Q1 (reference question):
Q1: "In paragraph II, what does 'they' (line 2) refer to?"
STEP 1: Find "they" in paragraph II, line 2.
Sentence: "She found that BOTH GROUPS had similar improvements."... wait, find the correct "they".
Read paragraph II from the start: "Dr. Jennifer Cho... tested TWO GROUPS of volunteers for 12 weeks. They did..."
STEP 2: Sentence before "they": "...tested two groups of volunteers for 12 weeks."
STEP 3: Closest noun = "two groups of volunteers" (or "volunteers").
STEP 4: Substitute: "The two groups of volunteers did seven minutes..." ✓ Makes sense. |
| --- |
| 1.  In paragraph II, what does "they" refer to? ("...the second group did seven minutes of intense exercise three times a day. They also reported...")
Give ONE answer. |
| --- |
| ANSWER KEY
Answer: the two groups of volunteers / the participants in the study.
P7: "they" → sentence before → "both groups" → substitute: "Both groups reported feeling more energetic." ✓ |

| THE FOREST EXPERIMENT
Scientists at Uppsala University in Sweden have been studying the effects of spending time in forests on mental health. In their latest experiment, they asked 200 volunteers to walk in a forest for one hour every day for two weeks. They found that stress levels fell by an average of 35% among participants. However, the scientists were careful to note that not all of these improvements were caused by the forest itself. "Some of the benefit may have come simply from the exercise of walking," explains Professor Anna Lindgren, who led the research. "We plan to repeat the experiment with a group that walks in a city — not a forest — to find out if the results are similar." |
| --- |
| T1.  What does "they" refer to in the sentence "They found that stress levels fell by an average of 35%"?
Give ONE answer. |
| --- |
| T2.  According to Professor Anna Lindgren, what does she plan to do next?
Give ONE answer. |
| --- |
| ANSWER KEY
T1: the scientists at Uppsala University.
P7: "They" → sentence before → "Scientists at Uppsala University" → substitute ✓
T2: repeat the experiment with a group that walks in a city, not a forest.
P3: Professor Lindgren → her plan. |
