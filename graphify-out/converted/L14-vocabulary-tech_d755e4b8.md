| TEACHER OVERVIEW — L14

LESSON 14  —  Vocabulary: Technology and Environment
Word families · Collocations · Notice → Understand → Adapt → Use

Objective
By the end of this lesson the student uses 20 technology and environment words in context and understands key word families and collocations.
Key insight
"Knowing a word is not enough. Knowing HOW it combines with other words (its collocations) is what makes writing sound natural. "An effective way to" — not "an effective method for". "Benefit from" — not "benefit of". These patterns are what examiners reward."
Timing plan

Common mistakes to watch for
· Student uses "research" as a verb: "I researched the topic" — correct! But "a research" (incorrect) → "research shows"
· Student writes "the effective" — needs a noun: "an effective way"
· Student confuses "affect" (verb) with "effect" (noun): "the effect of noise" / "noise affects sleep"
· Student writes "benefit of" — the collocation is "benefit from" |

| Word | Hebrew | In a question | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| research | מחקר | Research shows that sleep matters. | Uncountable — NO "a research". "Research shows / research suggests." |
| technology | טכנולוגיה | New technology is changing jobs. | Uncountable. "Technologies" = specific types. |
| environment | סביבה | The environment is under threat. | Natural world. "environmental" = adjective. |
| affect / effect | להשפיע / השפעה | Noise affects sleep. The effect is serious. | Affect = verb. Effect = noun. Common exam pair. |
| prevent | למנוע | Exercise prevents health problems. | To stop something from happening. |
| TEACHER
Show the 4 key collocations on the board:
"an effective way to" / "benefit from" / "research shows/suggests" / "have a positive/negative effect on"
Students copy these into their vocabulary notebook as ready-made phrases. |
