# Brand

- **Name:** EngUp (written `Eng` + teal `Up`), site www.engup.co.il, sender team@engup.co.il, support support@engup.co.il.
- **Motto:** מורה פרטי לאנגלית משלכם. Used in the site title, email footers, and wherever a one-line tagline is needed. Change it here first, then in `he.ts` (`home.metaTitle`) and `emails/auth/*.html`.
- **Voice:** Hebrew, plural "you" (אתם/משלכם), warm, brief, no hype. Never "optional" labels in settings; they appear only at sign-up.
- **Colors:** teal `#2A9D8F` (accent), `#23867B` (text on light), ink `#1F2937`, muted `#8B8F95`.
- **Logo:** `/icons/icon-192.png` plus the `EngUp` wordmark (Heebo 900).
- **Section headline (site only):** "קשה באימון. קל בבגרות." - a campaign line, not the motto.
