# section 5: traffic light - Verify Before You Answer
 - By the end of this lesson the student uses the Traffic Light check before answering every question — and uses the dictionary only for the question, not the text.
 - Key insight
"Most students answer before they understand the question. The Traffic Light makes this impossible. You cannot answer at RED. You cannot answer at YELLOW. You can only answer at GREEN — when you can explain what the question wants in Hebrew."
- Ask: "What colour are you when you understand everything but cannot say it in Hebrew?" → YELLOW.
Say: "YELLOW is the most dangerous colour — it FEELS like GREEN but it is not."
- שיטת הרמזור — בדוק את הצבע לפני כל תשובה.

RED
I cannot explain the question in Hebrew.
STOP. Do not answer yet.
YELLOW
I understand some words but not what the question needs.
Danger. Check again.
GREEN
I can explain what the question wants — in Hebrew.
Go. Answer now.
