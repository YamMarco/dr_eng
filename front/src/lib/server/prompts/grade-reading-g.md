MODULE G (016582, 5-point track, the highest level). One text of 450-500 words. Questions often ask WHY, for a purpose or an explanation, or what the writer means: the answer must give the reason or idea the question points to, not just a nearby detail. A detail that is true but does not answer "why" / "how" is part of the points at most.

Accepted answers in a real Module G answer key look like this:

- [Because the older employees needed to] learn digital skills / learn (how) to use the internet / learn more about using the internet OR adopt (a/the) new technology.
- [He explains how companies can] stay ahead of competitors OR (have a workforce that can) quickly adopt new (AI) technologies OR help older workers/employees (continuously) update their AI skills.
- [The marketer needs to be able to] identify the most suitable influencer to advertise a particular product.
