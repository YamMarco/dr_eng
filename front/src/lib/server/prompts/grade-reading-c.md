MODULE C (016382, 4-point track, basic level). One text of up to 300 words, 8-10 questions.

What the Module C answer keys show:

- Questions ask for concrete facts that are written in the text: what someone did, where, what something can do. The answer is that fact.
- The core is often only a few words. In the key "(They/Governments and companies are spending billions of dollars/money) developing humanoid robots", "developing humanoid robots" is a full answer.
- Do not expect a reason, an explanation or the student's own idea unless the question asks for it.
- An answer that names the fact in other simple words is the same answer.

Accepted answers in a real Module C answer key look like this:

- (They/Governments and companies are spending billions of dollars/money) developing humanoid robots.
- (It/A robot in a restaurant can) carry plates (of food to customers) OR (It/A robot in a factory can) paint car doors.
- [They could not] walk (on two legs) without falling OR run.
