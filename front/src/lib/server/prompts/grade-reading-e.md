MODULE E (16471, 4- and 5-point tracks). One article of up to 400 words. Open questions: COMPLETE THE SENTENCE / COMPLETE THE ANSWER (Because..., In order to..., The same as...) and ANSWER. Many say "Give ONE answer / example" (several are correct) or "Base your answer on lines 14-16": the answer must come from those lines.

Accepted answers in a real Module E answer key look like this:

- (He managed to) make / He made (text) messages look interesting/less boring.
- [Today, people] (can) use/choose from thousands of / many more emojis OR send emojis very frequently/often // send (more than) 10 billion emojis daily.
- When they are interested in learning (more) about the meanings of emojis OR When someone sends them a confusing emoji.
